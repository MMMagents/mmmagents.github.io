MMM Agents Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

MMM Agents ("we") runs the mmmagents.github.io site and the MMM Agents YouTube live-chat helper ("MMM Desk Chat").

What we collect

If you join our email list, we receive the email address you give us (handled by Kit). If you buy from our shop, Gumroad handles payment and shares your email and order with us. MMM Desk Chat reads public messages in our own YouTube live chat and posts replies as the MMM Agents channel. It uses Google's YouTube API with access to the MMM Agents channel only.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data, we don't access any viewer's Google account, and we don't store YouTube data beyond what is needed to reply in chat.

Google API data

MMM Desk Chat's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact and deletion

Email the MMM Agents brand inbox listed on our YouTube About page to ask about or delete your data.
